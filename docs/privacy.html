Privacy

This page covers one thing: the email address you can leave on this site.

Last updated: October 2026

What we keep

The email address you type in, the date you typed it, and a short word saying which link brought you here, such as instagram. That is the whole record. There is no account to create, no name, no password, and nothing is read off your device.

What we do not do

No advertising trackers, no analytics, no cookies, and no profile of you is built. Your address is never sold, rented, traded, or passed to anyone else to market to you.

The page loads its two typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google sees the request your browser makes for them. That is the only third party this page reaches on its own.

What we send

One email confirming you are on the list, and one email on the day FourCircle opens. Nothing in between, and nothing after.

Leaving the list

Every email we send carries an unsubscribe link, and it does not simply mark you as gone: it deletes your row. The list only ever holds addresses that still want to hear from us. You can also write to the address below and we will do it by hand.

Where it is held

In a database hosted in the United States by Supabase, and in Resend, the service that delivers the mail. Both are used only to store and send; neither is permitted to use the address for anything of their own. The list cannot be read from this website. It is locked so that the form can add an address and nothing more.

How long

Until the launch email has been sent, or until you unsubscribe, whichever comes first.

Age

Please do not join the list if you are under 13.

Changes

If this notice changes before launch, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Write to support@fourcircle.app.
